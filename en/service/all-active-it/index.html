Everything your club needs

From the first training session to the member list: clearly in one place.

Sign up or cancel with one tap

Members see their upcoming training sessions and matches and say with one tap whether they're coming. No more chats or lists.

Recurring training in one go

Plan a training session once and repeat it every week. If something changes, adjust the whole series or a single date.

Members and groups

Add members one by one or all at once from Excel. New members automatically join the training sessions of their group.

Attendance tracking

Coaches see in advance who is coming and record afterwards who was actually there.

Parent accounts

Parents sign their children up or out from their own account and switch between their children with one tap.

In your own club colours

Your logo, banner and colours. After logging in, members see their own club's branding.

For everyone in the club

Everyone sees exactly what they need, on the computer and on the phone.

Members and parents

  • The next event right on the home screen
  • Sign up or cancel in a few seconds
  • Parents switch between their children with one tap

Coaches

  • See who is coming to each session
  • Track attendance per session
  • Create and edit events, including recurring ones

Board

  • Manage the member list, groups and roles
  • Import members from Excel
  • See who hasn't accepted the invitation yet

The app for Android and iPhone is coming soon. Until then, everything already works on the phone through the website.

Get started in three steps

  1. Get in touch

    We set up your club and go through the options together.

  2. Add your members

  3. Plan your training
